umi_tools.py - Tools for UMI analyses

There are 6 tools:

  - whitelist
  - extract
  - group
  - dedup
  - count
  - count_tab

To get help on a specific tool, type:

    umi_tools <tool> --help

To use a specific tool, type::

    umi_tools <tool> [tool options] [tool arguments]
